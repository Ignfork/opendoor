// open.rs: the price is a discount off what the vault owes, never a fixed number
pub fn discounted_pay(owed_lamports: u64, discount_bps: u16) -> Result<u64> {
    require!((1..=9_999).contains(&discount_bps), DoorError::InvalidDiscount);
    let factor = 10_000u128 - u128::from(discount_bps);
    let pay = u128::from(owed_lamports) * factor / 10_000;
    u64::try_from(pay).map_err(|_| error!(DoorError::Overflow))
}
What is the line, exactly?

SOL lending reserves lend out deposits. When every loose SOL is lent, there is nothing to pay a withdrawal with, so the reserve files it as a ticket in a first-in, first-out queue. Tickets get paid as borrowers repay or liquidations land.

Why can't I just sell my ticket?

A ticket filed straight with the vault belongs to that wallet until it is paid. OpenDoor files the ticket from a program account made for one place, and keeps who holds that place on its own ledger. That is what lets the place change hands.

What does standing cost?

About 0.017 SOL of rent for a moment, for the place account, its token account and the ticket. Most of it comes back to the wallet that stood when the place closes, even if the place was sold. First-time standers can get it covered by the rent desk.

What if nobody buys my place?

Nothing is lost. You stay in line and the vault pays you in full when the line reaches you. The door fee only applies to a sale.

Where does $DOOR come in?

The 0.30% door fee on every sale is split between holders, buybacks and the rent desk. Holding more $DOOR lowers your own fee, down to zero at the Landlord tier.

Is this live?

Not yet. The program goes live with the token, and the CA is posted on X at @OpenDoorSol. Until then the calculators on this site run on numbers you type in.
